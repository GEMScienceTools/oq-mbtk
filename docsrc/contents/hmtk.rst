Hazard Modeller Toolkit (hmtk) module
###################################

The Hazard Modeller Toolkit is included in oq-mbtk under the
``openquake.hmtk`` namespace. Existing imports remain valid.

It provides earthquake catalogue parsing and selection, completeness
analysis, declustering, magnitude-frequency estimation, seismicity
smoothing, fault and source modelling, strain tools, and plotting.

`HMTK tutorial <https://github.com/GEMScienceTools/hmtk_docs/blob/master/hmtk_tutorial.pdf>`_
